## 1. 初始商品图

~~~text
Create a realistic sample product photograph for a tutorial demonstrating AI-assisted commercial product imagery. One clearly fictional, unbranded scented candle: a short, wide cylindrical translucent amber-glass jar with a single unlit black wick, a blank matte cream rectangular paper label on the front, and a brushed brass lid placed flat beside it. Product is fully visible and occupies about half the canvas. Neutral warm off-white seamless background and tabletop, soft even studio light, centered front three-quarter viewpoint, clean natural grounded shadow, realistic amber glass, restrained professional ecommerce photography. Landscape composition approximately 3:2. No lettering, no logo, no watermark, no other products, no illustration, no captions. Keep the jar and lid easy to identify for a subsequent background-only edit.
~~~

## 2. 参考第一张图进行场景修改

~~~text
Edit this exact fictional candle sample photograph into a refined lifestyle advertising image. Preserve the exact candle jar silhouette and scale, amber glass, unlit wick, front-facing blank cream rectangular label, and separate brushed brass lid from the reference. The product has no brand, no text. Change the background and lighting only: place the jar and lid on a subtly textured pale limestone table by a softly lit window, add a soft cream linen cloth behind and to the side, delicate leaf shadows from a nearby plant, and warm early morning sunlight. Minimal premium home fragrance campaign, photorealistic, tasteful, realistic contact shadows. The jar must be fully visible, label unobstructed, lid beside it. Landscape approximately 3:2. No people, no lettering, no new products, no logo, no watermarks.
~~~

人工检查时可比较瓶身形状、标签、烛芯和瓶盖。生成模型可能改变局部细节，真实商品必须继续与客户原图核对。
